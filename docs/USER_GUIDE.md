## 2. Student Experience

### Browsing & Enrolling in Courses
1. Navigate to **Courses** to view available fintech curriculums.
2. Filter by Category (*Digital Banking, Blockchain & Bakong, Regulatory & AML, Microfinance*).
3. View prices displayed in both **USD ($)** and **Cambodian Riel (៛)** at the 4,100 KHR benchmark rate.
4. Click **Enroll Now** (for free courses) or **Checkout with KHQR** (for premium courses).

### Paying with Bakong KHQR
1. On the checkout screen, select **KHQR** as your payment method.
2. An official Cambodian KHQR code will render with a 5-minute security countdown timer.
3. Open any Cambodian banking app (Bakong, ABA Mobile, Wing, ACLEDA, Sathapana) and scan the QR code.
4. The system automatically detects your payment within 3 seconds and redirects you to your course!
   *(In demo/testing mode, click **Simulate Success (Sandbox Demo)** to instantly activate your enrollment).*

### Learning in the Video Player
- **Speed Controls:** Choose from `0.75x`, `1.0x`, `1.25x`, `1.5x`, or `2.0x`.
- **Timestamped Notes:** Click **Take Note** at any moment in the video. Clicking your note later jumps the player straight to that exact second!
- **Auto-Advance:** When finishing a video, a 5-second countdown banner seamlessly transitions to the next lesson.
- **Sidecar Finance Hub:** Click the calculator drawer button to access financial formulas without navigating away from the video.

### Submitting Assignments & Taking Quizzes
- **Assignments:** Download assignment briefs, review the instructor's rubric, and submit PDF or code attachments via drag-and-drop.
- **Quizzes:** Timed interactive quizzes with real-time countdown. Receive instant question-by-question explanations upon submission.

### Earning & Verifying Certificates
Upon 100% course completion:
1. Navigate to **My Enrollments** or your completed course page.
2. Click **View Certificate** to open your official credential.
3. Every certificate features a cryptographically generated QR code linking to `/verify/:certificateNumber`.
4. Anyone can scan your certificate QR code to verify its authenticity, recipient name, and completion timestamp.

## 3. Interactive Cambodian Finance Hub (`/finance-tools`)

The platform includes 5 built-in financial tools:
1. **Loan / EMI Amortization:** Calculate monthly payments and principal-vs-interest breakdown for Cambodian loans.
2. **Compound Growth Calculator:** Project long-term savings growth with monthly deposits and annual returns.
3. **50/30/20 Budgeting Rule:** Break down your monthly income into Needs (50%), Wants (30%), and Savings (20%) in both USD and KHR.
4. **USD ↔ KHR Live Converter:** Instant bidirectional exchange calculator using the NBC 4,100 KHR benchmark rate.
5. **Cambodian Fintech Glossary:** Searchable encyclopedia of terms including KHQR, Bakong, NBC, CBC, MFI, and CAFIU AML/CFT laws.

## 4. Instructor Experience

### Creating & Managing Courses
1. Access the **Course Builder** from your instructor console.
2. Follow the **Course Completeness Score Meter** to ensure your course meets all accreditation criteria:
   - Course title, thumbnail, and short description
   - Module and lesson organization (videos and PDF attachments)
   - Syllabus outcomes and prerequisites
   - Practice quiz and pricing
3. Grade weighting categories (e.g. Quizzes 30%, Assignments 30%, Final 40%) can be configured under **Course Settings**.
4. Rubric builder allows instructors to establish clear criteria levels (e.g. Exemplary, Proficient, Developing).
